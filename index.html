What Explains Home Prices Across Massachusetts Towns?

R · Fall 2026

I merged Zillow home values with Census data for 346 Massachusetts towns and estimated log-log regressions with robust standard errors (adjusted R² of 0.70). A 10-point higher share of adults with a bachelor's degree was associated with roughly 10% higher home prices. Distance to Boston added little once income and education were controlled for.
